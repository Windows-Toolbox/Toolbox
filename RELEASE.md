Unpack the zip anywhere – a folder of its own, not inside *Program Files* – and double-click `Toolbox.exe`. Your
settings go into `data\` next to it. Programs come with one click under **Get programs** in the Toolbox, or with
**Add program…** from a folder you unpacked yourself.

The `.exe` is not signed (signing costs money). Windows SmartScreen may therefore warn once at the first start:
*More info → Run anyway*. Updates that the Toolbox downloads afterwards are checked against its own signature and
do not ask.
